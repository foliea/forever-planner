Complete the efficient quest rewards

  • ! [50] The Princess’s Surprise

    Start the Alliance princess chain with Royal Historian Archesonus beside Ironforge’s throne room. Continue through The Fate of the Kingdom, then accept this quest from Princess Moira inside BRD and return to King Magni in Ironforge.

    Reward: Songstone of Ironforge
  • ! [51] Hero of the Stormpike

    Take The Battle of Alterac from Prospector Stonehewer outside the Alliance AV entrance in Alterac Mountains (40.6, 79.6). Win an AV and complete the follow-up.

    Reward choice: Ice Barbed Spear
  • ! [54] Brought to Heel

    Available to Paladins in Forever from level 45. Start with The Burning Beast from a Weathered Etching, complete Chains That Bind, then continue with Bloodmage Lynnore in the Blasted Lands (50.6, 14.5). The exact Forever location of the starting etching is not yet documented.

    Reward: Infernal Lasso
